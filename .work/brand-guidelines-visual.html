06 · Guardrails

Evidence before publish

Do

Gunakan bukti visual sebenar. Semak entity, harga, stok, lokasi, rights dan CTA. Simpan versi yang diluluskan.

Jangan

Jangan mereka claim kesihatan, harga, berat, stok, ownership atau SKU. Jangan campur ABANGCOLEK dan Jerux tanpa mapping.

Workflow

Idea → draft → entity check → evidence → rights → founder approval → publish → metrics → learning.
